# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, give constructive feedback, and discuss the work rather than attacking people. Respect privacy and credit others' work.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Report concerns privately to support@ailesson.io. Maintainers will review reports confidentially where possible, consider the context, and may request corrections, remove content, restrict participation, or ban participants. Enforcement decisions can be appealed by replying to the same address with relevant context.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and spaces where someone represents this project.
